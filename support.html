Support

Questions, bugs, or a level that has you stuck

info@strangeglyph.com

Cairna is made by one person. There’s no ticket queue and no bot. Mail goes to a real inbox and gets a real reply, usually within a couple of days.

How do I play?

Tap a stack to lift it, then tap the dots to drop its stones one per square, bottom stone first. Your path can turn wherever the dots allow. Only the top stone of each square counts: make the board match the small goal pattern. Hold a stack to see the order its stones will drop.

What do the stars mean?

Every level has a proven fewest number of moves. Solve it in that many for three stars, within two more for two stars, or any way at all for one. A level solved with a hint can earn up to two stars.

What does that square do?

Tap any special square (water, grass, ice, a bloom and so on) with nothing on it and a short card explains what it does.

I’m stuck

Open Hint: a nudge shows which stack to lift, and Show next move draws the whole path. You start with three hints and earn more by solving levels in the fewest moves and by finishing passages. You can also watch a short video for a free hint, up to three a day.

How do I open the next passage?

Passages 1 to 3 are open from the start. Passage 4 opens when you finish the first three, and each one after opens when you finish the one before. You can also watch a short video to open passage 4, 5 or 6 right away.

My purchases are missing

Go to Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with. Expansions, stone sets and the Stone Vault come back on every device. Hints that were already used can’t be restored.

I need a refund

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and through your iCloud account if iCloud is on, so it can follow you to your other devices. There’s no Cairna account.

Sound, music and haptics

Use the speaker button on the Passages screen or in a level to change the volume or turn music off. Settings has separate switches for music, sound effects and haptics.

Accessibility

Turn on Settings › Color-blind outlines so light and dark stones never depend on color alone. Cairna follows Reduce Motion in iOS settings.

Something is wrong, or missing

Tell us at info@strangeglyph.com. Bug reports and “I wish it did X” are equally welcome. For a bug, your device model, iOS version, and the passage and level help a lot.
