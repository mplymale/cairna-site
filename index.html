How to play

One move. Three taps
you already understand.

There are no timers and nothing to memorize. The dots always show where the next stone can go.

01
Lift a stack

Tap any stack and all of its stones come up together.

02
Lay a path

Drop the stones one square at a time, bottom stone first. Your path can turn wherever the dots allow.

03
Match the pattern

Only the top stone of each square counts. Make the board match the goal, and the level is yours.

Every level has a proven fewest number of moves. Match it for three stars.

Features

Simple to start.
Satisfying to master.

Each passage adds one new idea, so the puzzles deepen without ever piling on rules.

Every level, proven

The fewest-moves target isn’t a guess. Each one is checked, so three stars always means you found the best way.

Squares with a will

Water you can’t cross, grass that holds, ice that slides, falls that carry, blooms that turn a stone over. Tap any one to see what it does.

Hints when you want them

A gentle nudge, or the whole next move. Earn more by playing well. Never needed, never pushed.

Build your cairns

Finish a passage and its stones fly off the board into a cairn. Earn every star to crown it, and use the stones you earn anywhere.

Sound you can feel

Pebble, glass, wood and ice each sound and tap differently. Quiet ambient music plays underneath, and you can turn any of it off.

Calm by design

No timers, no lives, no energy bars. Undo as often as you like. Color-blind outlines are one switch away.
