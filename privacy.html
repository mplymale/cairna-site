Privacy Policy

Strange Glyph, LLC  ·  Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

The short version. Cairna has no accounts and never asks who you are. Your progress lives on your device. We count how the game is played, anonymously, so we can make the puzzles better. Ads appear only when you choose to watch a video, and they are never personalized. We never sell your data.

Who we are

Cairna is made by Strange Glyph, LLC, a limited liability company registered in Georgia, USA, which is the data controller. Questions about this policy, or any request under it: info@strangeglyph.com.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, best moves, hints, stone choices and settings are stored on your device. There is no Cairna account and we never receive this information as something tied to you.

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress may be synced between your own devices through your iCloud account. That sync is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we cannot read it.

What we collect

Anonymous gameplay analytics

We use PostHog to learn how the game is played: which levels people finish, where they get stuck, how often hints are used, and when something goes wrong. Events are linked to a random identifier created on your device, not to you. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier, and we do not store IP addresses with these events.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, technical details such as the device model, iOS version and where in the app it happened may be sent so we can fix it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm what you have bought and restore it on your other devices. RevenueCat receives Apple’s purchase record and an anonymous app user identifier, not your name or Apple ID.

Advertising

Cairna shows ads only when you choose to watch one: to open a passage early, or to earn a hint. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. The videos are served by Google AdMob, set to non-personalized ads rated for a general audience.

To deliver and measure an ad, Google may receive technical information such as your device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and the fact that an ad was shown. Cairna does not ask to track you across other apps and websites, and does not share your advertising identifier for that purpose. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple’s leaderboards under your Game Center profile, under Apple’s privacy policy. It is optional and you can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Who else processes data

  • Apple — purchases, iCloud sync and Game Center. Privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — purchase records. Privacy policy.
  • PostHog — anonymous analytics and crash reports. Privacy policy.
  • Google (AdMob) — the videos you choose to watch. Privacy policy.

International transfers

Strange Glyph, LLC is a US business. Where any of our processors transfers data outside the EEA or UK, that transfer is made under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

How long we keep it

Anonymous analytics events are kept for up to [RETENTION] and then deleted. Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app or use Settings › Reset progress.

Your rights

Because we do not know who you are, there is very little about you for us to hold. You can reset all progress in the app at any time, and withdraw ad consent in Settings. If you are in the EEA or UK you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your local supervisory authority. Email info@strangeglyph.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Cairna is a family-friendly game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are non-personalized and rated for a general audience, and in-app purchases are protected by Apple’s purchase approval and Ask to Buy.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will update the date above and, where it affects you, tell you in the app.
